Geothermal Catalyst Dashboard

One-page illustration of the Emerging Climate Technology Framework (ECTF) applied to next-generation geothermal. Levers shift adoption timing, cost decline, and catalyzed emissions reductions versus the baseline case.

Default context (DOE Liftoff 2024)
3.7 GW online today. Next-gen resource expands US potential from ~40 GW to 5,000+ GW. 90-100 GW feasible by 2050, with up to ~300 GW in constrained scenarios.

Research notes
Learning rate: derive from historical cost vs cumulative capacity curves for similar geothermal wells or adjacent drilling technologies.
Starting cost: use current LCOE estimates from pilots, vendor quotes, and comparable projects (FOAK vs SOAK).
TAM: model addressable grid needs by region, firm capacity requirements, and siting/permitting constraints.
Premium to parity: discounted sum of green premium ($/MWh) over deployed MWh until parity (premium clipped at $0 thereafter).
